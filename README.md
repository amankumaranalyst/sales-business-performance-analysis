# Sales & Business Performance Analysis

End-to-end data analytics project analyzing regional sales performance using **Python** (data cleaning & EDA) and **Power BI** (interactive dashboard).

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Sales teams often lack a clear, data-driven understanding of regional performance, making it difficult to identify growth opportunities and optimize resources. This project aims to analyze and visualize regional sales data to uncover trends, evaluate profitability, and support strategic decision-making.

### What's the Business Question?

- Inconsistent revenue and profit performance across U.S. regions
- Lack of visibility into seasonal swings, top SKUs, and channel profitability
- **Goal:** Leverage 5 years of historical data to pinpoint growth levers and optimize strategy

---

## 🛠️ Approach / Solution

1. **Data Cleaning & EDA (Python)**
   - Cleaned and explored sales/order data using Pandas
   - Identified trends, seasonal patterns, and outliers using Matplotlib & Seaborn

2. **Dashboard Development (Power BI)**
   - Built an interactive Power BI dashboard covering executive, product/channel, geographic, and customer-level analysis
   - Used DAX measures for KPI calculations and dynamic reporting

---

## 📊 Key Areas Analyzed

- Revenue, Profit & Profit Margin
- Orders & Average Order Value
- Product Performance
- Sales Channel Profitability
- Customer & Regional (State-wise) Performance
- Seasonal Trends & Top-Performing SKUs

---

## 🧰 Tech Stack / Key Skills

`Python` · `Pandas` · `Matplotlib` · `Seaborn` · `Power BI` · `DAX` · `Data Cleaning` · `Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)` · `KPI Reporting`

---

## 📈 Outcome

Delivered an interactive, decision-ready dashboard that helps identify underperforming regions, profitable sales channels, and growth opportunities — supporting data-driven strategic decisions.
